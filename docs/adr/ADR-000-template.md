# ADR-NNN: Title

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-NNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Owner:** name or role
- **Linear:** PLA-NNN
- **Review trigger:** the condition or date that forces this decision to be re-examined

## Context

What situation forces a decision, and what constraints apply.

## Decision

The decision, stated so that it can be checked.

## Alternatives considered

Each alternative with why it was not chosen.

## Evidence and official sources

Links to current primary sources with the date checked, and to evidence files in `docs/sources`.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what work this creates, what it forbids.

## Unresolved risks

Anything that could invalidate the decision and who has to resolve it.
