# Provider terms, robots, and automation policy review

Review date for every row: **2026-09-02**. Method: official pages only; no address submitted; no
endpoint probed; no CAPTCHA, WAF, or rate limit bypassed. Full evidence and quotes (≤25 words) are
in [`provider-feasibility-matrix.md`](provider-feasibility-matrix.md). Robots.txt is an
operational signal, not authorization. `UNVERIFIED` means the official page could not be read on
the review date (403, timeout, or client-rendered) and must be re-checked in a browser.

Policy fields that are identical for every provider at tier 4 (`link_only`): approved transport =
user-directed official link; permitted data retention = none (no automated retrieval); required
attribution = nominative use of the brand name with a link to the official page; rate/concurrency
policy = not applicable (no automated traffic); disable/downgrade = configuration only
(`docs/runbooks/provider-disable.md`).

## Prohibited techniques (all providers, all tiers)

CAPTCHA solving or bypass; proxy rotation; browser fingerprint spoofing; defeating rate limits,
WAFs, or access controls; credential stuffing or account-only flow automation; scraping personal
customer data; fabricating user interactions or accepting terms on behalf of users; committing
live addresses, cookies, tokens, HARs, or raw checkout responses.

## Escalation

Questions in the last column are isolated for qualified legal review. Until answered, the
provider stays `link_only`. Answers are recorded by amending ADR-004 and this table with a new
review date.
